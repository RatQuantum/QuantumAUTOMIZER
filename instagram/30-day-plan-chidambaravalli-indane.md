# 30-Day Instagram Plan: @chidambaravalliindanegasservice

**Goal:** Become the most trusted LPG page in your area and turn followers into bookings, new connections and referrals.
**Format:** 1 Reel per day (Reels get the most reach to non-followers), plus Stories daily.
**Window:** Nov 1 – Nov 30, 2026 (includes Diwali on Nov 8).

> Note: this plan was written without viewing the live profile (Instagram was blocked in the work environment). Before Day 1, check the current bio, picture and best-performing old posts against Section 1.
> Verify every phone number / process step against the latest IOCL/Indane circulars before posting. Follow IOCL brand guidelines for logo use.

## 1. Profile setup (do this before Day 1)

### Profile picture
- **Best option:** a clean, close photo of the **owner or delivery staff in Indane uniform, smiling, holding a cylinder**. Faces beat logos for a local service business because people trust people.
- Background: plain, bright (shop wall or sky). Face fills ~60% of the circle. Check that it reads clearly at thumbnail size.
- Alternative: the Indane logo (per brand guidelines) with the agency name, but only if you want a "corporate" look. The face version usually gets more profile taps.

### Name field (30 characters, searchable)
Pick one. This is the bold line Instagram search matches, so it must contain the words people type.
- `Chidambaravalli Indane Gas LPG` (30)
- `Indane Gas | LPG Delivery` (25)
- `Indane Gas Chidambaravalli` (26)

### Bio (rewritten with the `/ig-profile` rubric: line 1 = who it's for + what changes, then proof, then one door)
```
Gas refill at your door in Chidambaravalli, booked in 30 seconds.
Authorised Indane LPG distributor since [YEAR]. Daily safety tips.
New connection? WhatsApp us 👇
```
Replace `[YEAR]` with your real year (or `Serving [N] families`). Never post a number that isn't true.

**Link:** ONE destination: your WhatsApp click-to-chat link (`wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX`). One door converts better than a menu of five links.

**Handle note:** `@chidambaravalliindanegasservice` is 31 characters and hard to say out loud. Keep it if customers already know it; if you ever change it, something like `@chidambaravalli.indane` is easier to say in a Reel.

### Account setup
- Switch to a **Business account**, category "Gas Station / Energy company / Local business".
- Add **Contact buttons** (Call, WhatsApp, Directions) and the shop address.
- **Story Highlights** (with simple matching covers): `Book Refill` · `New Connection` · `Safety` · `Offers` · `Reviews` · `Our Team` · `Office Hours`.
- Pin 3 posts after Week 1: (1) How to book a refill, (2) Your best safety reel, (3) Meet the team.

## 3. How to record

### Gear (phone is enough)
- Phone on a **cheap tripod** or stacked books, **vertical 9:16**, 1080×1920, 30fps (or 4K if the phone handles it).
- **Clip-on lavalier mic** (~₹300–800). Bad audio kills retention faster than bad video.
- Clean the lens before every shoot.

### Camera placement
| Shot type | Placement |
|---|---|
| Talking to camera | Phone at **eye level**, 1–1.5 m away, face in the top third (captions go in the middle/bottom) |
| Kitchen demo (regulator, stove) | **Over-the-shoulder** or **top-down** above the stove; second angle close-up on hands |
| Delivery / godown | Handheld walking shot at chest height, or tripod at a low angle so the cylinders look "big" |
| Customer reaction | Side angle (45°), customer looks at staff and not at the lens, which feels natural |

### Light & sound
- Face a window or shoot outdoors in **morning (8–10 am) or evening (4–5:30 pm)** light. Never with a window *behind* the speaker.
- Record in a quiet room; turn off fans and ceiling fans while speaking.

### Safety on camera (very important for your brand)
- Never show an open flame near a leaking cylinder, never "test" a leak with a matchstick, even as a "wrong way" demo. Show the wrong way with text/graphics only.
- Staff in uniform and safety shoes in delivery shots.

### Tone
- **Friendly elder brother/sister of the neighbourhood:** warm, quick, confident, never preachy.
- Speak in **Tamil (or the local spoken language), mixed with simple English words** people already use ("booking", "regulator", "KYC").
- Short sentences. One idea per reel. Smile at the start.
- Same opening line every time builds recognition, e.g. *"Vanakkam! Chidambaravalli Indane-la irundhu…"* (only after the hook, never as the first second).

## 4. The Triple Hook (use in every reel)

1. **Visual hook (0–1 s):** something moving or surprising in frame 1: cylinder thud onto the floor, hand twisting the regulator, close-up of a flame turning yellow, staff pointing at camera.
2. **Text hook (0–3 s):** big on-screen text, max 7 words, top-middle. Example: **"NEVER CHECK A LEAK WITH FIRE"** (no emoji: the hook scorer flags emoji at hook size)
3. **Verbal hook (0–3 s):** first spoken line creates curiosity or fear-of-missing-out. Example: *"Your gas is finishing early. Here's why."*

**Retention tricks**
- **Open loop:** "The 3rd tip saves the most gas." This keeps people watching to the end.
- **Pattern interrupt every 2–3 s:** cut, zoom-in (110%), new angle, or text pop.
- **Length:** 15–30 s for tips, 30–60 s for processes. Cut every pause, "umm" and breath.
- **Loop ending:** last line flows back into the first ("…and that's why you should never…" → loops to start).

## 5. Editing workflow (CapCut / Instagram Edits app)

1. Import clips → trim all silences (CapCut: *Auto cut / Remove pauses*).
2. **Auto captions** → style: bold white text, black outline, yellow highlight on the key word. Keep captions in the middle; avoid the bottom 20% (covered by the caption/UI) and the right edge (buttons).
3. **Text hook** on top for the first 3 s.
4. **Zoom punch-ins** on key words (100% → 115%).
5. **Transitions:** keep it simple. Use **hard cuts** mostly, a **whip/swipe** for "before → after", a **zoom transition** for reveals. No flashy 3D transitions.
6. **Sound effects (low volume):** "whoosh" on transitions, "pop" on text appearing, "ding ✅" on correct method, "buzzer ❌" on wrong method, "cash register" on gas-saving tips.
7. **Music:** a trending sound from the Reels audio library at **5–10% volume** under voice. For no-voice reels (B-roll, festival), use the trending audio at full volume.
8. **Brand ending (1 s):** logo/agency name + "Book now 👉 link in bio".
9. Export 1080p, upload with **"Upload at highest quality"** turned ON (Settings → Data usage and media quality).

## 6. How to upload (every day)

- **Best time:** test **7:00–8:30 am** (people cook breakfast, check phone) vs **7:00–9:00 pm**. After 10 days, check Insights and lock in the winner.
- **Cover image:** clean frame + 3–5 word title in big text, so the grid looks like a menu.
- **Caption:** hook line → 2–3 value lines → CTA → 3–5 hashtags.
- **Location tag:** your town every time (local reach).
- **Collab:** invite local pages/food bloggers/customers as **Collab** posts (it shows on both profiles).
- **Share to Story** right after posting with a poll or question sticker.
- **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** Ask a follow-up question in your reply.
- Share the reel to your **WhatsApp Status and customer groups**. Your existing customers are the fastest first viewers.

## 10. About "millions of views": an honest expectation
A local gas agency rarely reaches millions on every post. Your real win is **every family in your service area seeing you**. Reels with the best shot at going viral:
- **Universal safety/myth reels** (Day 2, 10, 14, 25): useful to anyone in India, so they spread beyond your town.
- **Relatable comedy** (Day 21, 26), using trending audio.
- **Emotional customer stories** (Day 24).
When one of these takes off, **make 3 follow-up versions within the week** (Part 2, different angle, reply-to-comment reel).
