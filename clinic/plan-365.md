# Krishnaa Clinic · 365-day plan (from Nov 20, 2026)

**730 reels = 2 a day.** 19 MBBS subjects × 20 patient-facing topics (`topics.py`), each topic told two ways:
- **A · morning · "Myth or fact / Warning sign"**: fast and punchy, the shareable one ("Never use earbuds inside your ear").
- **B · evening · "How your body works"**: calm wonder ("How your kidneys filter 180 litres a day").

Subjects rotate every day (no subject twice in a row) and a topic's two angles land ~6 months apart, so the second one is a fresh repackage (KB-7 #18). The full day-by-day plan is in `calendar.csv`.

## Reel formula (25-40 s; KB rules)
1. **0-1 s hook** with stakes: "Stop using earbuds. Here's what they do to your ear."
2. **By 5 s, lock-in**: "Stay till the end for the one thing to do instead."
3. **Body**: 3 beats, each a kinetic chip, number or icon. A "But…" re-hook at ~40%.
4. **Late payoff**: the fix or the wow fact.
5. **CTA (owner's rule, no selling)**: "Follow for one health fact every day, and share this with someone who needs it."
6. Small disclaimer line on the last frame.

## Phase plan
- **Months 1-3:** reach only (follow and share CTA). Track which subjects get the most shares and saves.
- **Month 4+:** double the winning subjects, and repackage the top 10 (KB-7 #18). Consider a soft "ask your questions in comments" Q&A reply format.
- Later (owner's call): appointment CTA, DM keyword automation.

## Doctor review
Every script goes to the owner first (a `script.md` in each reel folder). Only reviewed reels get rendered and posted.

## Weekly edit (every Sunday, both accounts)
A ~15 s beat-sync "edit" of the week's best hero shots (KB-12): clean re-renders without subtitles, cut on the song's beats with punch zooms, RGB glitch, flashes, neon outlines, shake and negative hits. `tools/reel-kit/edit/make_edit.py`. The song stays out of the repo; if Instagram mutes it, add the same sound from its audio library.
