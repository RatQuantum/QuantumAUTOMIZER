=== DAY 1 · REEL A (morning) · Your heart is NOT on the left ===
Your heart isn't where you think it is 🫀

It sits almost in the middle of your chest, behind the breastbone. Only about two thirds of it leans left. You feel the beat on the left because the tip of the heart (the apex) taps the chest wall there. And about 1 in 12,000 people are born with it pointing right (dextrocardia).

Why it matters: in CPR, press the centre of the chest, on the lower half of the breastbone. Not the left.

Follow for one health fact every day, and share this with someone who should know CPR 🙏

=== DAY 1 · REEL B (evening) · Squint: the brain switches an eye off ===
A squint isn't just about looks 👀

The brain makes one picture from two eyes. When the eyes point different ways, a young brain starts ignoring the turned eye, and that eye can stop developing good vision (lazy eye, or amblyopia). Treatment works best in early childhood, so after 4 months of age a squint is not something to wait out. Get it checked by an eye doctor.
