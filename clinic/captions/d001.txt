=== DAY 1 · REEL A (morning) · "My heart is failing!" ===
He was pressing the WRONG side of his chest 🫀

A man ran into my clinic holding the left side of his chest. But your heart sits almost in the middle, behind the breastbone. Only about two thirds of it leans left. You feel the beat on the left because the heart's tip (the apex) taps the chest wall there. About 1 in 12,000 people are born with the heart pointing right!

His ECG was normal, but chest pain should always be checked, never guessed. And in CPR, push hard in the centre of the chest, not the left.

=== DAY 1 · REEL B (evening) · "He'll grow out of it?" ===
His brain was switching one eye OFF 👀

A mother brought her 5-year-old with an eye turning in since babyhood. When the eyes point different ways, a young brain ignores the turned eye, and it can stop developing good vision: lazy eye (amblyopia). Glasses, patching the good eye and sometimes surgery help most in early childhood. A squint after 4 months of age is not something to wait out; get it checked by an eye doctor.
