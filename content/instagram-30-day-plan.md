# Lord Sri Krishna Temple: 30-Day Instagram Reel Plan

Window: Oct 31 – Nov 29, 2026, one Reel per day. Repurpose each Reel to YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels.
Festival days are approximate; confirm them against a panchang.

## How to use this file

In a Claude Code session on this repo, say: **"Build Day N from content/instagram-30-day-plan.md"**.
Put the photos and clips for that day in the HyperFrames project's `assets/` folder first.

## Brand template (every reel)

- 1080×1920 vertical, 15–60s
- Colours: saffron, gold, deep blue
- Keep text out of the top 15% and bottom 20% of the frame (Instagram UI)
- Audio: the temple's own kirtan or royalty-free audio from `media-use`. No film songs.
- Use original temple footage. Don't repost other creators' clips.
- End card: CTA for the day (see below)
- Handle: `@lordsrikrishnatemple` (confirm the exact handle)

## Structure of every reel

1. **Hook (0–3s):** written text on screen + spoken line + a strong visual
2. **Value:** one meaning, lesson or how-to
3. **CTA:** exactly one of the three types below

## CTA types

- **F (Follow):** "Follow for daily darshan from Lord Sri Krishna Temple."
- **P (Freebie):** "Comment MANTRA and we'll DM you the free Krishna mantra PDF." (auto-DM tool, e.g. ManyChat)
- **S (Service):** puja / archana in your name, annadanam or donation, astrology consultation, prasadam delivery. "Link in bio" or "DM BOOK".

Mix for the month: about 14 F, 10 P, 6 S.

## Niche keywords

Radha Krishna · Bal Gopal / Laddu Gopal · Bhagavad Gita motivation · Krishna leela stories · Hare Krishna kirtan · Krishna shringar darshan · Kartik Damodar month

Hashtags: 5–10 per post, mixing broad and niche (e.g. #radhekrishna #laddugopal #gitagyan #harekrishna).
