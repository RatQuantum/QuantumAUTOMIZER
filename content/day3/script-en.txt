Nobody tells you Kanha wakes before you.
Four AM. The whole town is asleep.
The bell rings once.
Suprabhatam, the song that wakes him.
Abhishekam, his morning bath.
Alankaram, fresh flowers and silk.
The first aarti is lit.
The doors open.
He's been up since four,
waiting to see you.
Follow for daily darshan with Kanha.
