## Picture 1 · frame 0, EPIC WIDE (no reference needed)
```
Create a photorealistic cinematic film still, vertical 9:16, ultra-wide epic landscape. A tiny ancient Indian cowherd village of thatched mud huts sits small in the lower third of a vast open plain. Above it towers a colossal, terrifying black storm that fills two thirds of the frame: churning black and deep-purple storm clouds, five or six jagged white-violet lightning bolts striking the ground, a dark grey wall of rain sweeping toward the village. At the far horizon, under the clouds, a last narrow band of fiery red-orange sunset glows. Warm orange oil lamps glow in the doorways of the huts. On a small mound in front of the village stands ONE small lone figure seen from behind, a villager in a simple white cloth holding a tiny glowing clay lamp up toward the storm; the figure is very small in the frame, which makes the storm feel enormous. Wind bends the trees and grass. High contrast: deep blacks, electric white lightning, fiery orange and red glows. Mood: dread, awe, the calm before disaster. Shot on a 24mm lens, low camera, cinematic film grain, like a still from a big-budget epic film. No text, no letters, no watermark.
```
