## The slots

**Tue 3 · Day 4 · Reel · Story · #21 Mid-Sentence Start (about 45 s)**
- On screen: "கண்ணன் ஏன் வெண்ணெய் திருடினான்?"
- First spoken line: "...யசோதா திரும்பிப் பார்த்தபோது, வெண்ணெய்ப் பானை காலியாக இருந்தது."
- Angle: the butter leela, told as a scene, then the traditional meaning: butter is what is churned out of
  a devoted heart, and Kanha takes only what is offered with love (Navaneeta Krishna).
- Pictures: child Kanha at a clay pot, Yashoda, the gopis' hanging pots (check every figure's dress).
- Voice: an expressive Tamil storyteller (Aarumugam again, or try a new one for a playful tone).
