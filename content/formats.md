# Reel formats (rotate, never repeat back to back)

The user, 6 Nov: "every reel feels the same… Please be creative." Days 7, 9 and 10 shared one skeleton (pain line → turn →
the same recorded Krishna sentence → the same "கிருஷ்ணா" ask, same font, same track). Rule from now on:
**no format, voice line, call to action, text style or music track may repeat in two consecutive reels**, and any one
format at most once in 5 reels. Log each reel's choices in the table at the bottom before building it.

| # | Format | What it is | Why it can work | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Story arc (Days 5, 7, 8, 9, 10) | pain → turn on the drop → Krishna's promise | proven once (Day 5) | 1-4 pictures/clips |
| B | **Pick a card** (Day 11) | 3 cards shuffled on the beat; pause and pick; each flips to a Gita promise | rewatches + "comment your number" | nothing but HyperFrames |
| C | Chat with Kanna | a phone chat: "கண்ணா, நீ இருக்கியா?" typed at 3 a.m., read receipts, then his reply | POV, highly sendable | nothing but HyperFrames |
| D | One verse, one minute | a single Gita verse: Sanskrit line, word-by-word Tamil meaning appearing on the beat, one-line takeaway | saves (teach) | a verse checked against the text |
| E | Did you know (temple) | 3 facts about the temple / a festival, real photos or footage | trust, local search | real photos from the temple |
| F | Kolam / line-drawing | Krishna's feet, a peacock feather or a flute drawn live in gold line art, then the meaning | ASMR, satisfying watch time | SVG paths |
| G | This or that / quiz | "Which of Krishna's names is yours?" by birth month / a 3-question quiz | comments | nothing but HyperFrames |
| H | Real darshan reveal | the 33.4K format: real footage, the reveal on the drop | the page's best reel ever | real footage from the temple |
| J | **Animated skit with a Krishna cameo** (Day 14) | original SVG characters in a modern scene; Krishna appears, helps, winks | relatable, shareable, a recurring "cameo" series | nothing but HyperFrames |
| I | Countdown / 108 names | rapid names on every beat, a stop on one with its meaning | rewatches | the names list |

Voice lines: never reuse the same recorded sentence in consecutive reels. Many formats need no voice at all.
CTAs to rotate: comment your number · send it to someone · save it for later · comment "கிருஷ்ணா" · follow for part 2.
Tracks: ask the user for a new track (and its BPM and drop) every 2-3 reels; the beat grid comes from the track.
