# Day 9 · Kurukshetra, the cosmic form · Nano Banana 2.1 pictures, then Google Flow clips

Make each picture in Gemini (9:16, full size), then in Flow use **Frames to Video** with it as the START frame and the
matching motion prompt. 2 versions each, send the best 4. No names of gods, places or characters in any prompt.
Picture 1 is frame 0 and the thumbnail. For pictures 3 and 4 attach your Day 7 Krishna picture.
For Krishna's forehead: the U mark must be **pure white** with a red centre line (Day 8 came out gold-outlined).

## Picture 1 · frame 0, EPIC WIDE (no reference)
```
Create a photorealistic cinematic film still, vertical 9:16, ultra-wide epic shot at dawn. An ancient Indian battlefield: on the left and on the right, two endless armies stretch to the horizon, thousands of warriors in bronze armour, war elephants, horses, hundreds of tall coloured banners and flags, spears glinting. Between them lies a wide empty plain of dust. In the exact centre of that empty plain stands ONE small ornate golden war chariot drawn by four white horses, tiny in the frame, which makes the armies feel enormous. Above, a huge blood-red rising sun glows through drifting dust and smoke, painting the whole sky fiery red-orange against deep shadowed ground. High contrast: deep black-brown shadows, blazing red and orange light, golden rim light on the dust. Mood: the silence before a great war, dread and awe. 24mm lens, low camera, film grain, like a still from a big-budget historical epic. No text, no letters, no watermark.
```
