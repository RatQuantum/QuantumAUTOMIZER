# Series 1 plan: a multi-part Krishna story where the comments choose

Status: **approved: "Arjuna's Charioteer"**, posting on Instagram and TikTok. Part 1 built.

## What the reference does (Blockframe-style "town monster" series, millions of views)

- **One world rule in the first line**: "In this town, the monster can only move in the dark." A rule is instantly clear, and it is a promise that it will break.
- **First-person kid narrator** ("me and my dad"): the viewer rides along as a character, not as an audience.
- **Constant escalation**: lights, generators, a clock (2:13 AM), sparks, a storm, someone falls, the monster stands over them.
- **Cliffhanger ending**: it stops mid-danger ("…the monster again." / "Why?").
- **~2 minutes long**, a caption word every beat, a new shot every 1-2 s.
- **Same characters and the same town every part**, so returning viewers recognise it at a glance.

## What the research says about series and choice stories

- Choose-your-own-adventure series on TikTok run on **comment votes** ("vote in the comments for the next scene") or on reply chains, and they pull far more comments than a normal post.
- What counts for a series is **part-to-part retention** (how many people who watched Part 1 come back for Part 2), not Part 1's views.
- **3-7 parts** is the usual sweet spot. Each part must make sense on its own and also reward people who saw the earlier ones.
- Instagram can now **link Reels into a series** ("Watch Part 2" prompts), and it shows skip rate for the first 3 seconds.
- Many of the specific numbers in these blog posts (for example, "series get 2.8x returning viewers") are unverified, so treat them as directional only.

## The proposal: "The Demons of Gokul"

**World rule (the hook line of every part):** "In Gokul, the demons never come as demons. They come as something you'd trust."

**Narrator:** Madhu, a 9-year-old cowherd boy (an original character), Kanha's best friend. Kanha is little Krishna. The viewer is Madhu.

**Why this story:** it is rooted in the Bhagavatam, where Kamsa sends a disguised demon after child Krishna again and again. That gives a ready set of episodes with real stakes. **Krishna's role and every demon's ending stay as scripture tells them.** The viewers only choose what *Madhu* does: where he goes, who he tells, what he notices. This makes the choice real without rewriting the sacred story.

| Part | Disguise (from the Bhagavatam) | Cliffhanger choice (A or B) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A sweet-voiced woman asks to hold baby Kanha (Putana) | A: run to warn Yashoda · B: stay and watch her |
| 2 | A sudden whirlwind lifts Kanha into the sky (Trinavarta) | A: chase the wind up the hill · B: ring the temple bell for help |
| 3 | A strange calf in the herd won't stop staring (Vatsasura) | A: follow the calf to the river · B: tell Kanha first |
| 4 | A giant white crane at the lake (Bakasura) | A: hide the other boys · B: throw stones to scare it |
| 5 | A cave shaped like a smiling mouth (Aghasura) | A: walk in with the herd · B: stop at the entrance |
| 6 | The finale: the cave closes, and Kanha does something no one expected | No choice; a payoff and a "Season 2?" vote |

**How a choice works in practice**
- The last 5 s of a part show two big cards, **A** and **B**, with "COMMENT A OR B · NEXT PART IN 24 HOURS".
- Pin our own comment ("A = …, B = …") so voters see the options.
- Also run an **Instagram Story poll** with the same two options, because a poll is one tap and gets far more votes than comments.
- Part N+1 opens with a 2-second recap, "You chose A…", then plays that branch. Only the winning branch gets made, so there is no wasted work.
- **Low-vote fallback:** our reels currently get 0-6 comments. If a vote gets fewer than ~10 votes, the account decides and says "It was close…". Over time the vote itself should raise comments, which is the point.

**Format for each part**
- 60-75 s (the reference is about 2 min; we're allowed up to 50 s for normal reels, but series parts earn the length).
- Title card "THE DEMONS OF GOKUL · PART N · YOU DECIDE".
- First-person narration in the kid's voice (a lighter Kokoro voice).
- Same Gokul set, same cast and same colours every part. Night scenes use lamps and fireflies for the eerie feel.
- Each part works alone (the rule is restated in the first line), and each part links to the previous one with Instagram's series link.

**Cadence:** one part a day as one of the five daily reels, so the other four keep the "Why we do what we do" series running. A 6-part season takes 6 days.

## Chosen: "Arjuna's Charioteer" (season 1)

**Hook line every part:** "On this battlefield, there was one chariot no arrow could stop, because God Himself was driving it."
**Narrator:** Ishan, 12, keeper of the chariot's four white horses (an original character; voice: Kokoro am_puck).
**Rule:** the Mahabharata's events and Krishna's words stay as scripture tells them. Votes only decide what Ishan does and sees.

| Part | Canon event | Choice at the end |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 1: the chariot drives between the armies; Arjuna drops his bow and weeps | A: run and pick up his bow · B: stay still and listen to Krishna |
| 2 | Krishna begins the Gita; then He shows His universal form (Vishvarupa) | A: look at the form · B: shut my eyes |
| 3 | Day 14: Jayadratha must fall before sunset; Krishna hides the sun with His chakra | A: tell Arjuna the sun is still up · B: keep the secret |
| 4 | Day 17: Karna's serpent arrow; Krishna presses the chariot into the earth and the arrow only takes Arjuna's crown | A: warn Arjuna · B: hold the horses steady |
| 5 | The last night: Krishna tells Arjuna to step down first, then the chariot bursts into flames | Finale: no choice; "Season 2?" vote |

Each part: 55-75 s, title "ARJUNA'S CHARIOTEER · PART N · YOU DECIDE", A/B cards in the last 8 s, "FOLLOW · PART N+1 TOMORROW".
Part N+1 opens with a 2-second recap: "You chose A…".
