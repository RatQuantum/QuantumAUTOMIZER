A mother tied up God, because He stole butter. 🙏

As the story goes, little Krishna and his friends climbed on each other's shoulders, broke the butter pots hanging from the roof, and fed the monkeys too. The gopis came running to complain to Yashoda every single day. But here's the secret. Krishna never stole from the rich houses. He went to the homes where the gopis sat churning butter, singing His name all morning. Why those homes? Because butter churned with love was the only thing He wanted.

That's why we offer makhan mishri to Krishna, especially on Janmashtami. He doesn't want gold. He wants what you make with love.
