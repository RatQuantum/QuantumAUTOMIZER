Every year, God sits on a swing, and devotees push Him for days. 🙏

As the story goes, in the rainy month of Shravan, the clouds came over Vrindavan, peacocks danced, and the gopis hung a swing from a kadamba tree. Radha and Krishna sat together, and the gopis pushed them higher and higher. But the swing went so high that Radha got scared, and held Krishna's hand tightly. did He stop the swing? No. He smiled, and said, as long as you hold my hand, you will never fall.

That's why we celebrate Jhulan Yatra, decorating swings with flowers for Radha and Krishna. Hold His hand, and life's ups and downs become a joy.
