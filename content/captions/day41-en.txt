Little Krishna once cried to His mother… because of a girl. 🎨

As the story goes, Krishna looked at His blue skin in the river, then at Radha, so fair and bright. He ran to Yashoda in tears: "Why am I so dark? She'll never play with me." Yashoda laughed and whispered a secret: "Go color her face. Any color you like."

So He grabbed a fistful of red, ran to Radha and painted her cheeks. She laughed and colored Him back, and soon the whole village was red, yellow, green and pink.

That's why we play Holi with colors. Love sees no color.
