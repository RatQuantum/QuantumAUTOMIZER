Why is Krishna called "Ranchhod", the one who ran from battle? 🏃

As the story goes, the fearsome Kalayavana attacked Mathura. He had a boon: no weapon could kill him. So Krishna did something strange. He turned around and ran. Kalayavana laughed and chased Him, out of the city, over the hills, into a dark cave.

Inside, someone was sleeping under Krishna's yellow shawl. Kalayavana kicked him... but it was King Muchukunda, who had a boon of his own: whoever woke him would burn. Muchukunda opened his eyes, and Kalayavana turned to ashes.

Krishna didn't need a weapon. He needed a plan.
