Why do grown men build human pyramids to break ONE pot? 🙌

Blame a five-year-old. In Gokul, little Krishna stole butter from every house, so the women hung the pots high, out of reach. Krishna just whistled for his friends. One knelt, one climbed on his shoulders, then another... and crack! Butter rained on the whole gang.

That's Dahi Handi. And that's why the climbers are still called Govindas: Govinda is His name.
