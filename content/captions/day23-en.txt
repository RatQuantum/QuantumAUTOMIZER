The most famous rakhi in history wasn't a thread. It was a torn sari. 🧵

As the story goes, at Indraprastha, Krishna cut His finger and it began to bleed. Everyone ran for a cloth. Nobody found one. Draupadi didn't think twice: she tore a strip from her own silk sari and tied it around His finger. "For every thread in this cloth," He said, "I will protect you."

Years later, in a cruel court, they tried to pull away her sari. She called His name, and the sari never ended. Thread for thread.
