Why did Yashoda almost faint when Krishna opened His mouth? 🌌

As the story goes, little Krishna's friends came running: "Mother! He's eating mud!" Yashoda grabbed Him. "Open your mouth, right now!" He shook His head... then opened His tiny mouth.

And inside, she saw the whole universe. Stars. Planets. Oceans. Mountains. The sun and the moon. And even herself, standing in Gokul, holding Him.

So Krishna smiled and made her forget it all. Because she didn't need God. She needed her little boy.
