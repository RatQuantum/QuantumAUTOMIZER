A dying warrior broke his own gold tooth to give it away. 🪙

As the story goes, Karna never said no to anyone who asked. On the last day of the war, he lay dying on the battlefield, and an old brahmin came and asked him for gold.

Karna had nothing left. So he broke his own gold tooth with a stone and offered it with a smile. The brahmin was Krishna, and He blessed Karna forever.

That's why we call Karna Daanveer. Giving is greatest when it costs you something.
