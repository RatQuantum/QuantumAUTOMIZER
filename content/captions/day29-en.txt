Why is Krishna called "Damodar"? Dam = rope. Udar = belly. 🪔

As the story goes, little Krishna broke a pot of butter and fed it to the monkeys. Mother Yashoda caught Him and tried to tie Him to a heavy grinding stone. The rope was two fingers too short. She added another. Still two fingers short. She tied every rope in the house. Always two fingers short.

No rope can hold God. But when He saw His mother tired and sweating, He let Himself be tied.

One finger is her effort. The other is His grace. He can only be held by love.
