A tiny boy asked a king for three steps of land, and took the whole universe. 🙏

As the story goes, King Bali was so powerful he had conquered the heavens. But he was also famous for his generosity. One day, a small brahmin boy named Vamana walked into his sacrifice. All I want is three steps of land. Bali laughed, and poured water to promise it. But then the boy began to grow. With one step he covered the earth. With the second, the sky. where was the third step? Bali bowed his head and said, place it on me.

Vishnu blessed him to rule the underworld, and to visit his people once every year. That's why Kerala celebrates Onam, to welcome King Bali home.
