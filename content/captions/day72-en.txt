A grandfather lay on a bed of arrows for fifty eight days, and refused to die. 🙏

As the story goes, on the tenth day of the great war, Bhishma fell, his body pierced by hundreds of Arjuna's arrows. The arrows held him above the ground. His head hung down. Give me a pillow, he asked. Arjuna shot three arrows under his head. Then Bhishma whispered, I'm thirsty. Arjuna shot an arrow into the earth, and a stream of the holy Ganga rose straight into his mouth. why didn't he die? He had the boon to choose his moment, and he waited for the sun to turn north.

Before he left, he taught the thousand names of Vishnu, with Krishna standing by his side. That's why we chant the Vishnu Sahasranama, and remember him on Bhishma Ashtami.
