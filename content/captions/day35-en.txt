Why do we light lamps on the day before Diwali? 🪔

As the story goes, the demon king Narakasura stole the earrings of the mother of the gods and locked away 16,000 princesses. Krishna flew out on His eagle Garuda, and beside Him, holding a bow, sat His queen Satyabhama. The battle was fierce, and Krishna fell wounded for a moment.

Who saved the day? Satyabhama. One arrow, and Narakasura fell. The princesses were free, and the people lit lamps all through the night.

That's Naraka Chaturdashi. Light wins over darkness.
