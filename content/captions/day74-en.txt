An old woman bit every berry before giving them to God, and He loved it. 🙏

As the story goes, Shabari waited for Lord Ram her whole life. Every morning she swept the path, and picked fresh berries, just in case he came that day. She grew old, but she never stopped waiting. Finally, Ram arrived. With shaking hands she offered the berries, but first she tasted each one. Lakshman was shocked. She's giving you half eaten fruit! why was she tasting them? To make sure only the sweetest berries reached her Lord.

Ram ate every single one, and said he had never tasted anything sweeter. That's why we say God tastes the love, not the food.
