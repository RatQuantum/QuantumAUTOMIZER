Every peacock in the forest danced until it dropped, and their king gave God the only thing he had. 🦚

As the story goes, Krishna climbed Govardhan Hill and played His flute. The clouds came, and the peacocks began to dance. They danced for hours, lost in the music, until they could not stand. But when the music stopped, the king of the peacocks came forward. He had no gold and no fruit. So he shook one feather loose, and laid it at Krishna's feet. Wait, a single feather? Krishna picked it up, and placed it on His crown, and said, I will wear this forever.

That's why Krishna wears a peacock feather. He never cares how big the gift is, only how much love it carries.
