A whirlwind snatched a baby from his mother's lap, and carried him into the sky. 🌪️

As the story goes, Yashoda was holding little Krishna in Gokul when a demon, Trinavarta, came as a roaring whirlwind. Dust filled the whole village. Nobody could see. When the wind stopped, Krishna was gone. But high up in the storm, the baby was holding the demon's neck. And He began to grow heavy. Heavier than a mountain. The demon could not fly. He could not breathe. Wait, what happened to the baby? The demon crashed onto a rock, and baby Krishna was sitting on his chest, laughing.

That's why we hold on to God when life spins. The storm may lift you, but He is heavier than any storm.
