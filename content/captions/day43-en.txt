A warrior gave up his own head… and Krishna gave him His own name. 🚩

As the story goes, before the great war, young Barbarik walked to Kurukshetra with just three arrows that could end the whole war in one minute. He had promised his mother: I will always fight for the side that is losing. Krishna knew that meant switching sides again and again until no one was left.

So Krishna asked him for a gift: his head. And Barbarik gave it, smiling. Krishna placed him on a hilltop to watch the whole war and blessed him: "In the age to come, you will be worshipped by my name. Shyam."

That's why millions pray to Khatu Shyam.
