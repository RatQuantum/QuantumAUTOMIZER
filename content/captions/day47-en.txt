An elephant was dying in a lake… with one flower in his trunk. 🪷

As the story goes, Gajendra, king of the elephants, went to drink with his family. Suddenly a crocodile grabbed his leg. He pulled. The crocodile pulled. For a thousand years, the story says. One by one, his family left him.

With his last breath, he plucked a lotus, raised it to the sky and called out to Narayana. Vishnu came flying on Garuda, so fast he forgot his crown. One spin of the chakra, and Gajendra was free.

That's why we offer God a lotus. When everyone leaves, He still comes.
