Krishna left Vrindavan… and He never played His flute again. 💔

As the story goes, one morning a golden chariot arrived to take Krishna to Mathura. The whole village ran after it. The cows stood still. Yashoda held the wheel. And Radha didn't cry. She just stood by the river and watched Him go.

Why didn't He come back? Duty. Mathura needed Him. Dwarka needed Him. The world needed Him. And years later, when He heard Radha was gone, He broke His flute.

That's why Radha and Krishna are worshipped together. They were never apart in their hearts.
