She had nothing left to give. One leaf was enough. 🍃

Draupadi's pot was empty, and ten thousand hungry sages were at her door. She called one name. He came, ate a single leaf stuck to the pot, and every sage felt full.

"Offer Me a leaf, a flower, a fruit, a little water, with love, and I accept it." Bhagavad Gita 9.26
