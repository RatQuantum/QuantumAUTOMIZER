Why is Krishna's flute hollow? Of all things, an empty stick of bamboo. 🪈

As the story goes, Krishna walked into a garden and every plant wanted to be chosen. The rose said, take me. The mango tree said, take me. But Krishna stopped at a tall bamboo: "Will you give me your life?" The bamboo trembled... then whispered, yes. He cut it, hollowed out its heart, pierced it with holes. And it made the sweetest music the world had ever heard.

When we empty ourselves of pride, He can play His music through us.
