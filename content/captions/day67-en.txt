A man who lived a sinful life was saved by calling his son's name. 🙏

As the story goes, Ajamila was once a good brahmin, but he lost his way and lived a life of wrongs. He had ten children, and he loved the youngest the most. His name was Narayana. At the moment of death, terrifying messengers of Yama came for him. Afraid, Ajamila cried out to his little boy, Narayana! what happened next? Shining messengers of Vishnu appeared, and stopped Yama's servants. He said the holy name.

Ajamila was given another chance, and spent the rest of his life in prayer. That's why we name our children after God. Every time we call them, we are calling Him.
