Why does Krishna wear a peacock feather instead of gold? 🦚

As the story goes, young Krishna played his flute in the forests of Vrindavan. The birds went quiet. The cows stopped chewing. Then the peacocks came, one, ten, a hundred, and danced for hours. When the music ended, the king of the peacocks walked up, bowed, and dropped one perfect feather at His feet. Krishna smiled, tucked it into His hair, and never took it off.

Not gold. A gift from a friend.
