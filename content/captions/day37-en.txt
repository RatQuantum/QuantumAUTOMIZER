Why did the greatest archer in the world drop his bow? 🏹

As the story goes, the war was about to begin. Arjuna said, "Krishna, take my chariot to the middle. I want to see who I'm fighting." And there he saw them: his grandfather, his teacher, his cousins. His hands shook. His bow, Gandiva, slipped from his fingers.

Krishna didn't scold him. Right there, between two armies, He began to speak. "Do your duty. Don't worry about the results." That talk became the Bhagavad Gita.

Life is a battlefield, and every one of us has dropped our bow.
