She gave Krishna fruit for a handful of grain… and her basket filled with jewels. 🧺

As the story goes, an old fruit seller walked through Gokul calling, "Fruits! Fruits for sale!" Little Krishna grabbed a handful of grain from the store and ran out. By the time He reached her, most of it had slipped through His tiny fingers. Just a few grains left.

She laughed and filled His arms with fruit anyway. When she lifted her basket, it was heavy: gold, rubies and pearls. Those few grains had become jewels.

That's why we give to God first, however small. He returns it a thousand times.
