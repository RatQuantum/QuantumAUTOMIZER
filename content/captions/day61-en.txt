Every year, a plant gets married to God, with a real wedding. 🙏

As the story goes, Vrinda was a woman so devoted that her prayers made her husband, the demon Jalandhar, impossible to defeat. He attacked even the gods. But the gods knew his strength came from her faith. So Vishnu came to her in disguise, her prayer broke, and Jalandhar fell. Vrinda was heartbroken. What did she do? She cursed Vishnu to become a stone, the Shaligram, and gave up her life. From her ashes grew a small plant, Tulsi.

Vishnu promised, I will never accept any offering without you. That's why every year we celebrate Tulsi Vivah, the wedding of Tulsi and the Shaligram.
