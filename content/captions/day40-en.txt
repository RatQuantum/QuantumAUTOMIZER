Why did Krishna marry 16,000 princesses? 👑

Remember Narakasura, who locked away 16,000 princesses? When Satyabhama defeated him, they were finally free. But they had nowhere to go. Their kingdoms wouldn't take them back. People whispered: "They were prisoners for years. Who will accept them now?"

Krishna said, "I will." He married every one of them, gave each a palace, and gave them His own name, so no one could ever whisper again.

Not for Himself. For their honor.
