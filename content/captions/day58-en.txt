A princess wrote a secret letter to God the night before her forced wedding. 💌

As the story goes, Princess Rukmini had loved Krishna since she first heard His name. But her brother arranged her wedding to Shishupala, a cruel king. So she wrote a secret letter: "If you don't come for me, I will give up my life." A trusted brahmin ran all the way to Dwarka with it.

The wedding day came, with no sign of Krishna. Rukmini walked to the temple of Goddess Ambika with tears in her eyes. As she stepped out, a golden chariot thundered in. Krishna reached out His hand, and she took it.

That's why girls pray to Krishna and Rukmini for the right life partner. God always answers a true heart.
