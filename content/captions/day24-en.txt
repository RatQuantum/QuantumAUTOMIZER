Why does Krishna get exactly 56 dishes? Not 50. Not 100. 56. 🍛

As the story goes, Vraj was cooking a giant feast for Indra, king of rain. Little Krishna said: the mountain feeds our cows, let's thank Govardhan instead. Indra was furious and sent a storm that would not stop. So Krishna lifted Govardhan on one little finger, and every family and every cow sheltered under it. For seven days. Without a single bite.

And little Krishna ate eight times a day. 8 meals × 7 days = 56. So when the sun came out, the mothers of Vraj cooked every meal He had missed.
