Krishna, or a whole army? Which would you choose? 🏹

As the story goes, before the great war, Duryodhana and Arjuna both rushed to Dwarka for help. Krishna was asleep. Duryodhana sat proudly at His head. Arjuna sat quietly at His feet. When Krishna opened His eyes, He saw Arjuna first.

So He made an offer: "On one side, my whole army, a million soldiers. On the other, just me. And I will not fight."

Arjuna chose Krishna without a second thought. Duryodhana laughed and took the army. Arjuna won the war.

Choose Krishna, and everything else follows.
