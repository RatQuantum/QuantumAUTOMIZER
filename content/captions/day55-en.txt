One proud bridge broke in seconds… and changed Arjuna's flag forever. 🚩

As the story goes, Arjuna met an old monkey by the sea and laughed: "Ram's bridge needed stones. I could build one with arrows." The monkey said, "If it breaks under me, you lose."

Arjuna shot a bridge of arrows across the water. The monkey stepped on it, and it cracked. Then Krishna came as a giant tortoise and held it from below, and this time it held. The monkey was Hanuman, and he bowed to Krishna.

That's why Hanuman sits on Arjuna's flag. Pride breaks, faith holds.
