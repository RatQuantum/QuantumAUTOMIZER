Narada thought he was God's greatest devotee, until Krishna pointed at a farmer. 🏺

As the story goes, Narada chanted "Narayana" all day, every day. One day he asked, "Lord, who loves you the most?" Krishna smiled and showed him a poor farmer who said God's name just three times a day. Narada laughed: only three times?

So Krishna gave him a test: carry this pot full of oil around the whole city without spilling a single drop. Narada walked slowly, eyes glued to the pot, and came back proud. Not one drop spilled. Then Krishna asked, "How many times did you think of me on the way?" Not even once. The farmer carried a family and his fields every day, and still remembered God three times.

That's why a small prayer every day matters.
