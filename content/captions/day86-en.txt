A man dipped his head into a river, and saw God sitting under the water. 🌊

As the story goes, Akrura came to take Krishna and Balarama to Mathura. On the way, he stopped at the Yamuna to pray. He dipped into the water, and there were the two boys, smiling at him, under the river. But that was impossible. He looked up, and they were still sitting in the chariot. He dipped again. This time he saw Vishnu himself, resting on a thousand headed serpent, in the water. Wait, how can one God be in two places? Akrura folded his hands and wept. He understood. Krishna is everywhere, even under a river.

That's why we bathe in holy rivers with faith. The water is not holy alone. It is holy because He is in it.
