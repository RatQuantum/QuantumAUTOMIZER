God fell sick, and the only cure was the dust from a devotee's feet. 🙏

As the story goes, in Dwarka, Krishna held His head and said, I have a terrible headache. Narada asked, what is the cure? Krishna said, the dust from the feet of someone who loves me. But the queens of Dwarka refused. To put our feet on God's head, that is a terrible sin! So Narada flew to Vrindavan, and told the gopis. Wait, did they hesitate? Not for a second. They poured the dust into his hands and said, if it helps Krishna, we will gladly take the sin.

Krishna's headache vanished. That's why we bow to the feet of devotees. Love that forgets itself is the greatest medicine.
