A father put his own son in the fire… and the fire refused to burn him. 🔥

As the story goes, the demon king Hiranyakashipu wanted the whole world to worship only him. But his little son Prahlad kept chanting Narayana. The king tried snakes, elephants, even a cliff. Prahlad just smiled.

So the king called his sister Holika, who had a shawl no fire could burn. She sat in a giant bonfire with Prahlad on her lap. Then the wind blew. The shawl flew off Holika and wrapped itself around Prahlad. Holika burned. Prahlad walked out, still chanting.

That's why we light the Holika bonfire the night before Holi. Faith walks through fire.
