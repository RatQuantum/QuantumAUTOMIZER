For one whole year, every calf in Vrindavan was actually Krishna. 🙏

As the story goes, Brahma, the creator, wanted to test the little cowherd boy. So while Krishna and His friends ate lunch by the river, Brahma hid all the calves and all the boys in a cave, and put them to sleep. But Krishna simply smiled, and became every missing calf and every missing boy. The same faces, the same voices, the same naughty smiles. did the mothers notice? They loved their children more than ever that year, without knowing why.

When Brahma came back a year later, he saw Krishna everywhere, and fell at His feet. That's why we say God lives in every being. Love them, and you are loving Him.
