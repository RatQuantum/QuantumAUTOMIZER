A hunter shot Krishna with an arrow, and Krishna comforted him. 🙏

As the story goes, near the end of His time on earth, Krishna was resting under a tree in the forest. A hunter named Jara saw His foot from far away, and thought it was a deer. He shot an arrow. When he ran closer, he saw it was Krishna, and fell to the ground crying. Forgive me, Lord. was Krishna angry? No. He smiled, and said, do not cry. This was meant to happen. You have done nothing wrong.

Krishna blessed the hunter, and left for His heavenly home. That's why we say, the greatest forgive even those who hurt them.
