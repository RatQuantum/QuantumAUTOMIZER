A mother cursed God… and He folded His hands and said, "I accept." 🕯️

As the story goes, after the great war, Gandhari had lost all hundred of her sons. Blindfolded and broken, she told Krishna, "You could have stopped this." Then she cursed Him: your family will fall like mine, and your golden city will sink into the sea.

Krishna didn't get angry. He bowed and said, "Mother, I accept. Your pain is real."

That's why Krishna is called the ocean of compassion. He doesn't run from our pain. He sits with it.
