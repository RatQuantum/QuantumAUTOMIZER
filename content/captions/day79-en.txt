Three of Krishna's closest friends became proud, and He humbled them all in one day. 🙏

As the story goes, Satyabhama thought she was the most beautiful queen. Garuda thought he was the fastest. And the Sudarshana chakra thought it was the strongest. So Krishna sent Garuda to bring Hanuman to Dwarka. Hanuman said, I'll come later. Garuda tried to force him, and was tossed far away. Then Hanuman reached Dwarka before Garuda, walked past the chakra at the gate, and asked, where is my Lord Ram? who did he see? Krishna appeared as Ram, but Satyabhama couldn't become Sita. Rukmini did.

All three were humbled. That's why we say, pride blinds us, and humility opens our eyes to God.
