Krishna asked a bamboo tree for something so painful, any other tree would have said no. 🎋

As the story goes, Krishna walked into a bamboo grove and asked one tall bamboo, will you give yourself to me? The bamboo said yes. Then Krishna said, I must cut you down, cut you open, and empty you completely. But the bamboo trembled. It would lose everything it was. Still it whispered, Lord, do as you wish. Wait, what happened to the bamboo? Krishna carved holes into it, held it to His lips, and the empty bamboo began to sing.

That's why the flute is closest to Krishna. When we empty ourselves of pride, His music flows through us.
