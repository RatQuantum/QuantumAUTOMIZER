Why does Krishna dance on a giant snake? 🐍

As the story goes, the serpent Kaliya poisoned the Yamuna. The water turned black, fish floated up, birds stopped singing. Little Krishna jumped from a kadamba tree right into the poison. Kaliya wrapped Him in his coils, but Krishna grew heavier and heavier, slipped free, and leapt onto the serpent's hoods. And He danced, and with every step a hood bowed down.

Did He destroy the snake? No. Kaliya's wives begged for mercy, and Krishna let him go.

Not to kill. To clean the river, and to forgive.
