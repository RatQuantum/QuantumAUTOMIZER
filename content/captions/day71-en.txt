A queen asked God to give her more pain, not less. 🙏

As the story goes, after the great war, Krishna was leaving for Dwarka. He turned to Kunti, the mother of the Pandavas, and said, ask me for anything. She had lost so much. Her home, her sons' kingdom, years in exile. Everyone expected her to ask for peace and comfort. But Kunti folded her hands and said, give me troubles, Krishna. why would anyone ask for that? Because in every trouble, you came to me. In comfort, I might forget you.

Krishna's eyes filled with tears. That's why we say, the hard times are when God is closest.
