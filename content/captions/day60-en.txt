A man has walked the earth for 5,000 years with a wound that never heals. 💎

As the story goes, after the great war, Ashwatthama was mad with rage. In the dead of night he attacked the Pandava camp while everyone slept. Still not satisfied, he fired the deadliest weapon, the Brahmastra, at an unborn child.

Krishna stepped in and saved the baby inside the womb. Then He took the shining gem from Ashwatthama's forehead and said, "You will wander alone for thousands of years, with a wound that never heals." Some say he still walks the forests today.

That's why we say: never let anger make your choices. Anger lasts a moment. Its pain lasts forever.
