Why did Krishna eat banana peels? 🍌

As the story goes, before the great war Krishna came to Hastinapur as a messenger of peace. Duryodhana laid out a royal feast: gold plates, fifty dishes. But Krishna walked right past the palace, to the small home of Vidura, a humble minister.

Vidura's wife was so overjoyed to see Him that she peeled the bananas, threw the bananas away, and handed Him the peels. And Krishna ate every one, smiling.

When Vidura gasped, Krishna said: nothing has ever tasted sweeter, because it was given with love.

God doesn't count what's on the plate.
