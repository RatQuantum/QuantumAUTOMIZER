Krishna swore He'd never lift a weapon… then broke His own promise. 🛞

As the story goes, in the great war, old Bhishma was unstoppable, and Arjuna couldn't fight his own grandfather with his full heart. But Bhishma had vowed to make Krishna pick up a weapon.

So Krishna leapt off the chariot, lifted a broken wheel and ran at Bhishma. Bhishma dropped his bow and smiled. Krishna broke His own promise to keep His devotee's.

That's why we say God bends for His devotee.
