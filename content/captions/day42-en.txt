Krishna was in terrible pain, and only one cure could save Him. Nobody would give it. 🙏

As the story goes, Krishna held His head in Dwarka. "The dust from the feet of someone who truly loves me. Place it on my head." The queens gasped: our dust on God's head? A sin. Never.

Narada ran to Vrindavan. The gopis didn't think for a second. "If it helps Krishna, let us carry the sin." The moment the dust touched His head, the pain was gone.

That's why the dust of Vrindavan is sacred. Love that forgets itself.
