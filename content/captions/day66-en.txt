A father asked, is your God in this pillar too? 🙏

As the story goes, Hiranyakashipu had a boon. No man or animal could kill him, not inside or outside, not by day or night, not on the ground or in the sky, by no weapon. He felt he could never die. His son Prahlad still chanted Narayana. Enraged, the king pointed at a pillar. Is He in there too? He is everywhere, said Prahlad. The king smashed the pillar. What came out? Narasimha, half man, half lion. At twilight, on a doorstep, on His lap, with His claws.

Every condition of the boon was met, and the demon was gone. That's why we celebrate Narasimha Jayanti. God will find a way to protect His devotee.
