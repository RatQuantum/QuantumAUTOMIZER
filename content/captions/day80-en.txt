Five thousand years ago, God gave one conversation that still answers every question. 🙏

As the story goes, on the battlefield of Kurukshetra, Arjuna lost all hope. Krishna spoke to him for just a few minutes, seven hundred verses about duty, fear, the soul, and love. But those verses didn't stay on the battlefield. They traveled across the world. Gandhi, Einstein, and millions more read them in their hardest moments. what is that book? The Bhagavad Gita, and the day Krishna spoke it is Gita Jayanti.

That's why we read one verse of the Gita every day. Do your duty, and leave the results to Him.
