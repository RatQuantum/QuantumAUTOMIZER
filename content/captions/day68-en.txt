On one full moon night, Krishna danced with every gopi at the same time. 🙏

As the story goes, on the night of Sharad Purnima, Krishna played His flute in the forest of Vrindavan. Every gopi left everything behind, and ran to Him under the moonlight. But there was one Krishna, and hundreds of gopis. So He did something only God can do. He became as many Krishnas as there were gopis, one beside each of them, dancing in a great circle. Did each gopi know? Each one felt He was dancing with her alone.

That's why on Sharad Purnima, we leave kheer under the full moon, and we dance the raas. God has enough love for every single one of us.
