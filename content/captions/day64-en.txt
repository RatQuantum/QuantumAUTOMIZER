A five year old boy became a star in the sky that never moves. 🙏

As the story goes, little Dhruva wanted to sit on his father's lap, but his stepmother pulled him away. Only a son of mine can sit there. Crying, he ran to his mother, who said, only God can give you a place no one can take. So the little boy walked alone into the forest. Narada tried to send him home, but Dhruva refused. He stood on one leg, and chanted, Om Namo Bhagavate Vasudevaya. what happened then? The whole universe began to shake, and Vishnu appeared before him.

Dhruva was given a place forever, as Dhruva Tara, the pole star, the one star that never moves. That's why we show children the pole star. Faith that doesn't move, moves God.
