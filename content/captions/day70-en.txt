A great scholar went to teach village girls about God, and came back crying. 🙏

As the story goes, after Krishna left for Mathura, He sent His wise friend Uddhava to Vrindavan with a message. Tell the gopis to meditate on me. I am everywhere. Uddhava came full of knowledge and big words. But the gopis didn't want lessons. They wept for Krishna, sang His name, and saw Him in every flower and every cloud. who was the student now? Uddhava fell at their feet. I came to teach, and I learned what love is.

He even prayed to be born as a blade of grass in Vrindavan, just to be touched by their feet. That's why we say, love is greater than knowledge.
