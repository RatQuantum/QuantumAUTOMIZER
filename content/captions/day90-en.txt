The king of heaven bowed to a cowherd boy, and a cow from heaven gave that boy a new name. 🐄

As the story goes, after seven days of storm, Indra saw that Krishna had held Govardhan Hill and saved every cow in Vrindavan. Indra came down, ashamed of his pride, and folded his hands before the little boy. But Indra did not come alone. With him came Surabhi, the mother of all cows. She poured her milk over Krishna, and bathed Him like a king. Wait, what did she call Him? Govinda. The one who protects the cows.

That's why we call Him Govinda, and why serving a cow is serving Him. Every cow He saved, He still loves.
