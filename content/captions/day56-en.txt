On one night every year, millions of people refuse to look at the moon. 🌙

As the story goes, Ganesha tripped after a big feast and the moon laughed at him. So Ganesha cursed it: whoever looks at you on my day will be falsely accused. Years later, on that very night, Krishna saw the moon, just its reflection in a cup of milk.

Soon a priceless jewel, the Syamantaka, went missing, and all of Dwarka whispered, "Krishna stole it." Krishna didn't argue. He followed the trail into the forest, to a dark cave, and fought a mighty bear for twenty-eight days. The bear was Jambavan, the old hero from Lord Ram's army. When he recognised Krishna, he fell at His feet and gave back the jewel.

That's why we don't look at the moon on Ganesh Chaturthi. And if we do, we hear this story to clear our name.
