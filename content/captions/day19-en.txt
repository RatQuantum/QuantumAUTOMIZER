Why is Krishna's food always offered with ONE tulsi leaf? 🌿

Satyabhama put the whole treasury on a scale against Krishna. The scale didn't move. Then Rukmini placed a single tulsi leaf, with His name on her lips, and the scale tipped.

Krishna can't be bought. But He can be loved. That's why His food is still offered with a tulsi leaf, and why at Guruvayur, devotees still give their weight in bananas (tulabharam).
