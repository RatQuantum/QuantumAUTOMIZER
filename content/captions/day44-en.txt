Once a year, God leaves His temple… because His little sister asked. 🛕

As the story goes, in Puri, Subhadra looked out and said, "Brothers, I want to see the city." So Jagannath and Balabhadra built three giant chariots: 16 wheels for Jagannath, 14 for Balabhadra, 12 for Subhadra.

Who pulls them? Not horses. Not kings. Us. Lakhs of people pull the ropes together, and even the king sweeps the road with a golden broom.

That's Rath Yatra. Once a year, God comes out to see His people, and anyone can touch the rope.
