Why did Krishna forgive 100 insults? 💯

As the story goes, Prince Shishupala was born with a third eye and extra arms. A voice from the sky said: the one whose touch makes them vanish will one day end his life. When Krishna held the baby, they vanished. His mother begged, "Please spare my son." So Krishna promised: "I will forgive him a hundred times."

Years later, at a royal ceremony, Shishupala insulted Krishna again and again. Krishna kept counting, calmly. 98. 99. 100.

At 101, He raised His finger, and the Sudarshan Chakra flew.

God's patience is endless, but it has a limit.
