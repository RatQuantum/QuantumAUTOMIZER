Everyone laughed at her bent back… until Krishna touched her. ✨

As the story goes, in Mathura lived Kubja, a woman bent almost in half. She made sandalwood paste for the cruel King Kamsa, and people mocked her every day. One morning a blue boy stopped her on the road: "Will you give me some of that sandalwood?" Everyone gasped. That was the king's paste.

Kubja didn't hesitate. She smeared it on His forehead with love. Krishna stepped on her toes, lifted her chin, and in one gentle pull her back was straight.

That's why we offer chandan to Krishna. What you give with love, He returns a thousand times.
