A python so big the boys thought it was a cave, and they walked straight into its mouth. 🐍

As the story goes, Kamsa sent the demon Aghasura. He stretched out as a giant python, his mouth wide open like a dark cave. The cowherd boys laughed, it's just a cave! And they ran inside with their calves. But the mouth began to close. The boys were trapped in the dark. Krishna could have stayed outside. Instead, He walked into the python's mouth too. Wait, why would He do that? Inside, Krishna grew bigger and bigger, until the python could not breathe, and burst open. Every boy walked out alive.

That's why we stay calm inside trouble. Krishna does not watch from outside. He walks in with us.
