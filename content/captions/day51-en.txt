God has stood on a brick for thousands of years… and never sat down. 🙏

As the story goes, Pundalik served his old parents day and night, washing their feet, feeding them by hand. One night, Krishna Himself knocked at his door.

Pundalik didn't stop pressing his father's feet. He threw a brick outside and said, "Stand on this and wait." He made God wait. And Krishna smiled, put His hands on His hips, and stood on that brick. He never left.

That's why Vitthal still stands on a brick in Pandharpur. Serving your parents is worship.
