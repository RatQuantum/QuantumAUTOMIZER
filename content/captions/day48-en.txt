A queen was handed a cup of poison… and she drank it singing. 🎶

As the story goes, Meera was a princess who loved Krishna since she was a little girl. She danced and sang for Him in the temple every single day. The royal family was furious. A queen, singing in the street? So they sent her a golden cup. "Drink this. It's nectar." It was poison.

Meera knew. She smiled, offered the cup to Krishna first, and drank every drop. Nothing happened. The poison turned to nectar, and she kept singing.

That's why we sing bhajans. When you sing to Him with your whole heart, nothing can harm you.
