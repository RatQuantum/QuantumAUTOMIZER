Why do temples stay awake till midnight on Janmashtami? 🌙

Because that's exactly when He came. A cruel king, Kamsa, had locked Devaki and Vasudeva in a prison. At midnight, Krishna was born. The chains fell open. The guards fell asleep. The doors swung wide. Vasudeva carried the baby through a raging Yamuna in the storm, and a great serpent spread its hoods over Him like an umbrella.

So every year, at the stroke of twelve, the conch sounds and the cradle swings.
