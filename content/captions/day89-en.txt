A crane as tall as a house swallowed Krishna whole, and the boys screamed. 🦢

As the story goes, the cowherd boys took their calves to drink at the lake. There stood a white crane, bigger than any tree. It was the demon Bakasura. With one snap of its beak, it swallowed Krishna. But inside its throat, Krishna began to burn, like fire. The crane coughed, choked, and spat Him out onto the grass. Wait, was Krishna hurt? Not a scratch. He caught the beak with both hands, and split it open like a blade of grass.

That's why the brave do not fear size. What looks huge to us is a blade of grass to Him.
