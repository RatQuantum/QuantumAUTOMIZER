A boy learned to break the deadliest army formation before he was even born. 🛞

As the story goes, Arjuna was telling his pregnant wife Subhadra how to enter the Chakravyuha, a spinning wheel of soldiers no one could escape. The baby in her womb was listening. But halfway through, Subhadra fell asleep. So the baby learned how to get in, but not how to get out.

Sixteen years later, that baby was Abhimanyu. On the thirteenth day of the war, he alone broke into the Chakravyuha. He fought seven great warriors at once, and when his sword broke, he fought on with a chariot wheel. He never came out, but no one has ever forgotten him.

That's why mothers listen to stories of courage and God while they're pregnant. The child is already listening.
