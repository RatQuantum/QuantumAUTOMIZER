Why does Lord Jagannath have no hands, and those big round eyes? 🙏

As the story goes, King Indradyumna of Puri wanted a statue of the Lord. An old carpenter appeared: "I will carve it. Lock me in for 21 days. Nobody opens the door." Days passed, the chisel echoed... then on day fifteen, silence. The worried queen opened the door. The carpenter had vanished. The statues were unfinished: no hands, no feet, just big round eyes.

That night the Lord came to the king in a dream: "Keep me like this. With no hands, I still hold you. With these big eyes, I see everyone."
