Five husbands, a hundred cousins, and only one person saved her. 🙏

As the story goes, the Pandavas lost everything in a game of dice, even their wife Draupadi. Dushasana dragged her into the royal court, and began pulling her sari in front of everyone. The elders looked away. Her husbands bowed their heads. Draupadi held on, until she let go and raised both hands. Govinda! what happened? Dushasana pulled and pulled, but the sari never ended. Mountains of cloth piled up, until he fell exhausted.

Krishna protected her honour, without even being in the room. That's why we say, when no one is with you, call His name.
