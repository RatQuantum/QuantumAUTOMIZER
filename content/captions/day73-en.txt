A king had seven days to live, and he spent them listening to a story. 🙏

As the story goes, King Parikshit, Arjuna's grandson, was cursed by a sage's son. In seven days, the serpent Takshaka will bite you. He didn't panic. He gave up his throne, and sat by the river Ganga. The young sage Shukadeva arrived, and for seven days and nights, he told the king the stories of Krishna. The king didn't eat. He didn't sleep. Was he afraid on the seventh day? No. His heart was full of Krishna, and death had nothing left to take.

That story is the Srimad Bhagavatam. That's why we hold the Bhagavat Saptah, listening for seven days.
