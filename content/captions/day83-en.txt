For seven days, little Krishna did not eat a single bite, and His mother never forgot it. 🍛

As the story goes, Indra flooded Vrindavan with rain. Krishna lifted Govardhan Hill on His little finger, and held it for seven days and nights. The whole village sheltered under it. But Krishna did not eat, not even once. When the rain stopped, Mother Yashoda counted. Her son ate eight times a day. Wait, eight meals, for seven days? That is fifty six meals He missed. So the village cooked all fifty six at once, a mountain of food, an Annakut.

That's why we offer Chappan Bhog, fifty six dishes. Every one is a mother's love, paying back His hunger.
