Why did Arjuna's chariot burst into flames? 🔥

As the story goes, after 18 days of war, Arjuna had won. Krishna stopped the chariot and said, "Arjuna, get down first." Arjuna was surprised: the charioteer always gets down first. But he obeyed. Then Hanuman, on the flag, bowed and vanished. Krishna stepped down... and the chariot burst into flames.

Why? For 18 days, the deadliest weapons had hit that chariot. Only Krishna sitting on it had held them back.

As long as He holds the reins, nothing can touch you.
