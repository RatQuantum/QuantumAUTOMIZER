Why is Krishna blue? 💙

As the story goes, when Krishna was a tiny baby in Gokul, a beautiful woman named Putana walked in and offered to feed Him. But she was a demoness, and her milk was poison. Baby Krishna drank and drank until He drew the life out of her, and the whole village saw the giant she really was.

Some say the poison turned Him blue. But the sages say something deeper: the sky is blue. The ocean is blue. Anything endless looks blue to our eyes.

He is as endless as the sky.
