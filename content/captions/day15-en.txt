He came. Every time. 🦚

When Draupadi stood alone in the silent court, He came.
When the storm hit Vrindavan, He lifted the mountain.
When Sudama came with nothing but a handful of rice, He ran to the door.
When Arjuna broke on the battlefield, He spoke the Gita.

He still does.
