Krishna was hungry, the priests refused Him, and their wives ran to feed Him. 🙏

As the story goes, one day the cowherd boys were hungry. Krishna sent them to some priests doing a big fire sacrifice nearby. Ask them for food. But the priests ignored them, busy with their rituals. Then the boys went to the priests' wives. The moment they heard Krishna was hungry, they packed every dish they had and ran into the forest, even though their husbands tried to stop them. What did Krishna do? He ate their food with joy, and blessed them.

Later, the priests realised that God was standing right there, and they had missed Him. That's why we say, prasadam is offered with love, not just with rules.
