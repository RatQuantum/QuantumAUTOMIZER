Why do we never go to a temple empty-handed? Even a banana. A flower. A spoon of sugar. 🍌🌸

As the story goes, Krishna's best friend at school was Sudama. Years later, Krishna was the king of Dwarka, and Sudama was so poor his children went to bed hungry. His wife tied a handful of flattened rice in an old cloth and sent him to see his friend. The guards laughed. But Krishna ran out barefoot, hugged him, grabbed that tiny bundle and ate it like a feast.

Sudama never asked for help. He walked home with nothing, and found a palace where his hut had stood.

It's not the size of the gift. It's the love.
