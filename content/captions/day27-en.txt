Why do we call Krishna "Govinda"? It wasn't His birth name. 🐄

As the story goes, after Krishna held up Govardhan for seven days, Indra, king of rain, finally understood and came down from the sky, ashamed. Behind him walked Surabhi, the mother of all cows. She poured her milk over little Krishna like a waterfall. Then Indra bowed and gave Him a new name: Govinda, the protector of cows.

So every time we sing "Govinda", we're thanking the boy who protected the cows.
