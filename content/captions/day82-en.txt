All the gold in a palace could not lift God, but one green leaf did. 🌿

As the story goes, Queen Satyabhama wanted to prove her love for Krishna was the greatest. Narada told her, weigh Him against your wealth. So Krishna sat on one side of a giant scale. She piled gold, jewels, crowns on the other. But the scale did not move. Not one inch. Her pride broke, and she called Queen Rukmini. Wait, what did Rukmini bring? No gold at all. Just one Tulsi leaf, with a prayer. She placed it on the scale, and the side with Krishna rose into the air.

That's why one Tulsi leaf is enough for God. He is weighed only by love.
