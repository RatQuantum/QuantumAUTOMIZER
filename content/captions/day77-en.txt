A cruel king invited two boys to a wrestling match, to kill them. 🙏

As the story goes, Kamsa invited Krishna and Balarama to Mathura for a festival. But it was a trap. At the gate stood a mad elephant, and in the arena, his giant wrestlers, Chanura and Mushtika. The crowd gasped. They are just boys! But Krishna and Balarama stepped into the ring smiling. They defeated the giants, and the whole city cheered. what about Kamsa? Krishna leapt onto his high throne, and ended his reign of fear.

Then He freed His parents, Devaki and Vasudeva, from prison. That's why we say, fear always loses in the end.
