# Day 7 · Nano Banana 2.1 prompts (Gemini app or Flow, Google AI Pro)

How to use:
1. In Gemini, attach the reference picture named under each prompt, paste the prompt, and ask for a **9:16 vertical** image.
   Download it at full size (2K).
2. Make picture 1 first. Use YOUR picture 1 (not the old one) as the reference for pictures 2 and 3, so the queen
   has the same face in every shot.
3. Then in Flow use **Frames to Video** with each picture as the START frame and the short motion prompt in
   content/day7/flow-prompts.md. A start frame keeps the face far better than Ingredients.

Rules: no names of gods or characters (Google blocks "third-party content"); she is always fully and modestly
dressed; Krishna wears the white U mark with a red centre line, never horizontal lines.

References:
