# Day 18: "One leaf was enough" · Google Flow prompts (7 clips)

The story is Draupadi's akshaya patra in the forest exile. Her pot is empty, ten thousand hungry sages (Durvasa and his
disciples) are coming, she calls Krishna, and He eats one leaf stuck to the pot, which satisfies every sage.
Krishna's line is Bhagavad Gita 9.26.

**How to make them (same as Days 5-8):**
- In Flow, choose **Veo 3.1**, **portrait 9:16**, 8 s.
- For clips 4, 5 and 7, add your **Krishna image from Day 6** as an ingredient, so it's the same face as before.
- Make clip 2 first, then use its last frame as the start frame for clip 3, so the woman stays the same.
- Veo's own sound doesn't matter, because I replace it. Faces don't need to be close-ups.
- Send me all 7 clips (2 takes of any you're unsure about). I'll cut them, grade them, add the new voices and render.

Each prompt avoids names on purpose (the rules say names trigger Flow's block), and every one is written to hold a
person's face at a medium distance.

---

**1 · HOOK (epic wide, the thumbnail)**
Epic wide cinematic shot at dusk: a small thatched hut on a riverbank at the edge of an ancient forest, and a long
procession of hundreds of ascetic monks with matted hair and saffron cloth walking along the river toward the hut,
raising golden dust. A blazing red and orange sky, smoke-like clouds, the river reflecting fire-coloured light. One
small figure of a woman in an ochre sari stands alone at the hut's door, tiny in the frame. Slow push-in, deep shadows,
high contrast, epic scale, photorealistic, 35mm film look.

**2 · The empty pot**
Inside a simple forest hut at dusk, lit by one clay oil lamp: a young Indian woman in a plain ochre cotton sari, long
black hair tied back, kneels and tilts a large empty copper cooking pot toward the lamp. It is washed completely clean
and empty. Her face fills with dread. Through the open doorway behind her, the dark silhouettes of many monks are
approaching against a red sky. Medium shot, warm lamp light and cool shadows, photorealistic, cinematic.

**3 · She calls (start from the last frame of clip 2)**
The same young woman in the ochre sari, still kneeling beside the empty copper pot, closes her eyes, joins her palms at
her chest and whispers a prayer, a single tear running down her cheek. The oil lamp flame flickers and the cloth
curtain at the doorway lifts in a sudden warm breeze. Medium shot from the side, soft golden light, photorealistic,
emotional, cinematic.

**4 · He comes (add your Krishna image)**
The doorway of the forest hut fills with soft golden light, and a young man with luminous blue skin steps in, smiling
gently. He wears a gold crown with a single peacock feather, a yellow silk dhoti, a garland of fresh flowers, gold
earrings, and a white U-shaped tilak with a thin red line in the centre on his forehead. Dust glows in the light
around him. Medium-wide shot, slow push-in, warm and divine, photorealistic, cinematic.

**5 · One leaf (insert, hands only)**
Close-up of hands beside a large empty copper cooking pot on a clay floor, warm oil-lamp light: a hand with luminous
blue skin, gold rings and a gold bracelet, gently picks a single small green leaf stuck to the inside rim of the pot
and lifts it toward the camera, slowly, as if it is precious. Shallow depth of field, photorealistic, cinematic.

**6 · Every sage is full (wide)**
At a riverbank at sunset, hundreds of ascetic monks in saffron cloth, standing waist-deep in the river for their
evening bath, suddenly stop, look at one another and place their hands on their stomachs, surprised and smiling, as if
they have just eaten a great feast. An old monk with long matted grey hair and a white beard laughs in wonder. Golden
backlight on the water, wide shot, photorealistic, cinematic.

**7 · The promise (add your Krishna image)**
Golden hour inside the forest hut: the young man with luminous blue skin, gold crown with a peacock feather, flower
garland and a white U-shaped tilak with a red line, holds a single small green leaf in his fingers and looks into the
camera with a warm, reassuring smile. Soft rim light, floating dust, gentle slow push-in. Medium shot,
photorealistic, cinematic.
