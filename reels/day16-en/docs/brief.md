# Brief: Day 16 (English): Your birth month, His name

- **The one thing the viewer keeps:** "My month's name for Krishna is ___", and they comment it.
- **Format:** G, this or that / quiz (new; the last five reels were C, D, J, J and K).
- **Length:** about 30 s. No track file, so it's paced to the voice and to reading time (rules 7g, 7h).
- **Track:** any calm flute or veena track the user picks in Instagram. Not ZAKHELO, which Day 15 used.
- **Assets:** none, everything is drawn in code (a golden wheel of months, Tanjore-style cards in ruby, emerald and gold leaf).
- **Sources:** traditional names of Krishna and their common meanings (Govinda, Madhava, Keshava, Gopala, Mukunda,
  Damodara, Shyam, Vasudeva, Achyuta, Hari, Mohana, Janardana). The month-to-name pairing is a devotional game, not a
  scripture claim. The caption says so.
- **Hard lines:** no astrology claims, no character art, nothing rushed (each page stays 4.3 s).
- **One call to action:** comment your name.
