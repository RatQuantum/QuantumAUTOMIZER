# Brief: Day 18 (English): One leaf was enough

- **The one thing the viewer keeps:** whatever little I have today is enough for Him.
- **Format:** A, story arc with real-looking characters (rule 17), last used on Day 10.
- **Story:** Draupadi's akshaya patra (Mahabharata, Vana Parva, forest exile): her pot is empty after the meal, the
  sage Durvasa arrives with ten thousand disciples expecting food, she prays to Krishna, He eats a single leaf stuck to
  the pot, and the sages bathing in the river suddenly feel full.
- **Krishna's line:** Bhagavad Gita 9.26 (patram pushpam phalam toyam): a leaf, a flower, a fruit, water offered
  with love, I accept.
- **Hook:** an epic wide dusk shot, hundreds of monks walking toward one small hut, with the line "One day… you'll have
  nothing left to give."
- **Voices:** Chatterbox (rule 18): narrator, Draupadi's whisper, Krishna.
- **Footage:** 7 Flow clips (content/day18/flow-prompts.md), made by the user.
- **CTA:** comment "Krishna" (Day 5's CTA; not used since Day 10).
